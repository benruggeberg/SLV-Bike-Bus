SLE Bike Bus

A group bike ride to San Lorenzo Valley Elementary. Grown-ups ride, kids ride along.

Leaves
8:00am from the corner of Cooper St & Farmer St
Arrives
At SLE 8:10–8:15
When
Every school day in October
How to join

How to join

  1. Create a free Love to Ride Santa Cruz account.
  2. Join the SLE Bike Bus group on Love to Ride.
  3. Sign up for morning text updates. TODO(Ben): text service and join code (e.g. "Text @CODE to 81010").
  4. Show up at Cooper St & Farmer St by 8:00am with your kids and your bike.
  5. Log your bike bus rides on Love to Ride for Biketober.

Route and schedule

About 1 mile, 10–15 minutes. One start point: no pickups along the way.

  1. Start: corner of Cooper St & Farmer St Leaves 8:00am
  2. Cross the Fall Creek pedestrian bridge
  3. Hwy 9 shoulder from Fall Creek Dr to SLV High School
  4. Through the SLV High parking lot
  5. Arrive at SLV Elementary 8:10–8:15am

Hwy 9 construction is ongoing near SLE, so the route may change. If it does, the notice at the top of this page will say so.

Who can ride and what to bring

Adults ride. Kids ride as passengers on cargo bikes or in trailers pulled by bikes. Each adult brings their own kids.

Before you leave home

  • Helmets on everyone
  • Brakes work
  • Tires pumped
  • Battery charged
  • Lights on
  • Kids seated and buckled the way your bike's passenger setup requires

Safety tips

  • Arrive a few minutes early so the bus can leave on time.
  • Ride single file on the Hwy 9 shoulder and stay behind the lead rider.
  • Leave a bike length of space. No passing within the group.
  • Call out hazards to riders behind you: "car back," "slowing," "stopping."
  • Slow down and yield to people walking on the Fall Creek bridge and in the high school lot.
  • Wear bright or reflective clothing, and run your lights even in daylight.
