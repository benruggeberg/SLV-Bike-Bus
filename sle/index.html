SLE Bike Bus

A group bike ride to San Lorenzo Valley Elementary. Grown-ups ride, kids ride along.

Leaves
8:00am from the corner of Cooper St & Farmer St
Arrives
At SLE 8:10–8:15
When
Every school day in October

How to join

  1. Create a free Love to Ride Santa Cruz account.
  2. Join the SLE Bike Bus group on Love to Ride.
  3. Show up at Cooper St & Farmer St by 8:00am with your kids and your bike.
  4. Log your bike bus rides on Love to Ride for Biketober.

Route and schedule

About 1 mile, 10–15 minutes. One start point: no pickups along the way.

  1. Start: corner of Cooper St & Farmer St Leaves 8:00am
  2. Cross the Fall Creek pedestrian bridge
  3. Hwy 9 shoulder from Fall Creek Dr to SLV High School
  4. Through the SLV High parking lot
  5. Arrive at SLV Elementary 8:10–8:15am

Hwy 9 construction is ongoing near SLE, so the route may change. If it does, the notice at the top of this page will say so.

Ride ready

Who can ride

Adults ride. Kids ride as passengers on cargo bikes or in trailers pulled by bikes. Each adult brings their own kids.

Before you leave home

All set. See you at Cooper St & Farmer St!

FAQ

What if I'm running late?

The bus leaves at 8:00am so everyone gets to school before the bell. It can't wait.

Can I join partway along the route?

Not right now. Everyone starts at Cooper St & Farmer St.

What happens if it rains?

We ride rain or shine. Light rain is part of the adventure: bring rain gear for you and a cover or poncho for the kids.

We cancel when riding isn't safe: heavy rain, strong wind, lightning, wildfire smoke, or road trouble like flooding or trees down on Hwy 9. If we cancel, a notice goes up at the top of this page by 7:30am. No notice means we're riding.

And it's always your call to skip a day.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's a free activity organized by parents.

Does it run after October?

If there's enough interest, we'd love to run it all school year!

Do I have to ride every day?

No. Join when it works for you.

How do I help lead?

Email hello@slvbikebus.org and we'll tell you what's involved.
