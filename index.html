Ride to school together

A bike bus is a group of families riding to school together on a set route and schedule, like a school bus on bikes. Grown-ups ride cargo bikes or pull kid trailers.

Routes

  • SLE Bike Bus: to San Lorenzo Valley Elementary. Leaves 8:00am from Cooper St & Farmer St, every school day in October.

Riding is voluntary and at your own risk. Each adult is responsible for their own bike, equipment, and children. Read the full disclaimer.
